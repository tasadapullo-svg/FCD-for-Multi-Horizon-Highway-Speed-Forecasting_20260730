SCI figure export package

Each of the 7 uploaded figures is supplied in four formats:
1. PNG at 300 dpi
2. PNG at 600 dpi
3. TIFF at 300 dpi (lossless LZW compression)
4. TIFF at 600 dpi (lossless LZW compression)

Export policy:
- 300 dpi files preserve the original pixel dimensions and store 300 dpi metadata.
- 600 dpi files are resampled to 2x width and 2x height with Lanczos filtering and store 600 dpi metadata, preserving the same nominal print size as the 300 dpi version.
- Transparent areas are flattened to white for robust journal production workflows.
