## Data provenance — new candidate
This study evaluates provider-reported road speeds obtained through the TomTom Traffic API for a single Pan Borneo Highway study corridor in Sarawak. The archived study covers 51 observation nodes from 8 December 2025 to 28 February 2026. Provider speed estimates and their quality indicators are the study inputs; they are not independent colocated measurements of true traffic speed. The current API documentation supports field interpretation but does not retrospectively establish the historical account terms or collection success.

## Data completeness — new candidate

The frozen audit reports 60.161036% returned-slot availability and 59.874551% quality-qualified coverage. The untraceable 97.30% figure is not retained. These availability measures do not establish live-service latency or deployment reliability.

## Threats to validity — new candidate
The evaluation is retrospective and limited to one corridor, and historical test outcomes have previously been exposed. Dependence-aware inference retains the whole spatial field within resampled dates, but does not remove the limitations of a finite 41-date test period, overlapping forecast horizons and changing fitted models across folds. Provider-reported speeds lack independent synchronous ground truth. Independent external validation, spatial-neighbor, Transformer and convolutional-recurrent model experiments were not conducted. Current OSM connectivity does not verify the historical highway mainline or its approximate 130 km length; 15 candidate nodes require author review. Original model results and the independent revision implementation must remain separate, and unavailable corrected Ridge paired results cannot be replaced by older numerical results.
